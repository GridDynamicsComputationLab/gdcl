Our goal is clear: stable and secure power grids with power electronics at their core.

The Grid Dynamics and Computation Laboratory studies the control, dynamics and stability of converter-infeed power systems. As HVDC links, renewables and storage connect through power electronic converters, grids behave in new ways. We build the models, methods and tools that keep them stable.

About the lab ›
Our research

From converter control to system-level stability

We model HVDC grids and hybrid AC/DC systems, design robust converter controls, and use eigenvalue and frequency-domain methods to find interactions and stability margins, including in multi-vendor and black-box systems.

Read more ›
Our lab

A laboratory-scale MMC prototype

Theory is tested in hardware. We are building a laboratory-scale modular multilevel converter to validate small-signal models, compare capacitor-voltage balancing strategies and test controllers in unbalanced and weak AC conditions.
