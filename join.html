Join the lab

Open to motivated students and researchers in power systems, HVDC and MMC.

Work on the problems that keep tomorrow's grids stable.

We welcome motivated undergraduate and postgraduate students interested in converter-infeed power systems, HVDC and MMC. Funded positions such as PhD, Junior Research Fellow and Project Associate are announced on the News page with their application forms.

Ways to join

Choose the route that matches where you are.

PhD, MS and M.Tech

Research degrees through the IIT Indore admission process, on HVDC, MMC and converter stability topics.

Project positions

Junior Research Fellow and Project Associate roles on sponsored projects with GRID-INDIA, ANRF and international partners.

B.Tech projects and interns

Final-year projects for IIT Indore students and summer internships for students from other institutes.

Collaborations

Joint research with universities, research institutes and industry in India and abroad.

How to apply

Three steps, in this order.

  1. Submit your CV

    Fill in the CV submission form with your background and interests.

  2. Check open positions

    Funded positions and their deadlines are posted on the News page with their own application forms.

  3. Write to the PI

    For questions about fit or topics, email lokesh@iiti.ac.in.
